🛒 E-Commerce App

📱 About The App
A Flutter-based E-Commerce App built with Clean Architecture that allows users to browse products, manage favorites, and handle shopping carts with a complete authentication system including email verification.
The app focuses on scalability, maintainability, and a seamless shopping experience.

✨ Features
🔐 Complete Authentication

Email/Password Registration & Login

Email verification with OTP codes

Secure token-based authentication

🏠 Home & Browsing

Dynamic banner carousel

Product categories browsing

Featured products display

🛍️ Shopping Cart

Add/remove products from cart

Real-time cart updates

Quantity management

❤️ Favorites System

Add and remove products from favorites

View saved favorite products

📦 Category Management

Browse products by categories

Category-specific product listings
